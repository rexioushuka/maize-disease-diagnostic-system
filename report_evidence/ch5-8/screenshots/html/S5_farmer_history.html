How to photograph a maize leaf

A good photograph gives a much better diagnosis. Follow these four steps.

  1. Pick one leaf that clearly shows the symptoms.
  2. Photograph it in daylight, avoiding shadows and glare.
  3. Fill the frame with the leaf and keep the camera steady.
  4. Use a plain background where possible and avoid blur.
